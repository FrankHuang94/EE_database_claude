# Practitioner Layer

This is the material for using engineering knowledge in M&A, venture and strategy work. It draws on the Practitioner lens section of every chapter, on [Course 28](../courses/28-technical-diligence-and-leadership-fluency/README.md) for the method, and on [Capstone C4](../capstones/C4-diligence-memo/README.md) for a worked memo.

| Folder / file | Contents |
|---|---|
| [playbooks/](playbooks/README.md) | Nine domain field guides: the questions that decide a deal, first-principles checks with the chapters that derive them, evidence ladders, red and green flags, a 90-minute meeting plan and a document request list |
| [question-banks/](question-banks/README.md) | Every chapter's Marketing vs Reality box and questions for engineering leaders, with strong and evasive answers, collected by domain. These files are generated from the chapters by `tools/build_practitioner.py`, and CI checks that they are current |
| [case-studies/](case-studies/README.md) | Real engineering decisions reconstructed from public sources: what was decided, the physics and economics behind it, and what a diligence team should have asked |
| [scenarios/](scenarios/README.md) | An index of the 26 conversation scenarios in [28-10](../courses/28-technical-diligence-and-leadership-fluency/chapters/28-10-conversation-scenarios.md), sorted by domain and linked to the playbooks |
| [translation-table.md](translation-table.md) | Business ↔ engineering translation: cost, time, customer value, quality and market access, each as a formula with its chapter |

**How the pieces fit.**

1. Frame the decision with [28-01](../courses/28-technical-diligence-and-leadership-fluency/chapters/28-01-technology-evaluation-framework.md).
2. Prepare with the domain playbook.
3. Draw extra questions from the question bank.
4. Rehearse with the scenarios.
5. Translate the findings into value with the translation table.
6. Write the memo in the shape of C4.

**Rules that carry over from the curriculum.**

- Every perishable fact is dated and cited, and claims are tagged **[Established]**, **[Industry estimate]** or **[Contested]**.
- Public information only.
- The playbooks and the translation table hold formulas and checks, not new numbers. Numbers come from the chapters, with their dates.
