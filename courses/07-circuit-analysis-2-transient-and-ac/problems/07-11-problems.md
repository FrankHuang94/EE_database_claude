# Problems 07-11 · Three-Phase Circuits

Chapter: [07-11](../chapters/07-11-three-phase-circuits.md) · Worked solutions: [07-11 solutions](../solutions/07-11-solutions.md)

**Tiers:** **[A]** concept/recall · **[B]** standard exam level · **[C]** challenge/design.
**Conventions:** RMS phasors; positive (abc) sequence with $\mathbf{V}_{an}$ at 0° unless stated; "V" ratings are line-to-line. Answers to 3 or 4 significant figures unless exact.

Suggested approach: do all [A] and [B] problems before looking at any solution. Attempt each [C] problem for at least 20 minutes before reading its solution.

---

## Problems

### Problem 07-11.1 [A]

**Line and phase.** (a) Find the line-to-line voltage of Y systems with 120 V and 277 V line-to-neutral. (b) Find the line-to-neutral voltage of a 400 V system. (c) A balanced Δ load carries 10 A in each phase; find the line current.

### Problem 07-11.2 [A]

**Line-voltage phasors.** With $\mathbf{V}_{an} = 277\angle0°$ V, write $\mathbf{V}_{bn}$ and $\mathbf{V}_{cn}$, and compute $\mathbf{V}_{bc}$ and $\mathbf{V}_{ca}$.

### Problem 07-11.3 [A]

**Δ to Y.** A balanced Δ load has $30 + j15$ Ω per phase. Find the equivalent Y impedance.

### Problem 07-11.4 [B]

**A 208 V load.** A balanced Y load of $12 + j9$ Ω per phase is supplied at 208 V. Find the phase voltage, the line current (magnitude and angle), and the real and reactive power and power factor.

### Problem 07-11.5 [B]

**A Δ-connected motor.** A three-phase motor draws 40 kW at power factor 0.85 lagging from a 480 V supply, with its windings in Δ. Find the line current, the winding (phase) current and the reactive power.

### Problem 07-11.6 [B]

**A Δ load through a line.** A balanced Δ load of $24 + j18$ Ω per phase is fed from a 400 V source through lines of $0.5 + j1$ Ω each. Using the Y equivalent, find the line current, the load's line voltage, the Δ phase current and the load's real power.

### Problem 07-11.7 [B]

**Unbalanced with a neutral.** A 120/208 V four-wire supply feeds 12 Ω from phase $a$ to neutral, 12 Ω from $b$ to neutral, and an inductive reactance $j12$ Ω from $c$ to neutral. Find the current in phase $c$ and the neutral current.

### Problem 07-11.8 [B]

**Harmonics and the neutral.** Each phase of a balanced load draws 10 A (RMS) at the fundamental, 4 A of third harmonic and 2 A of fifth. Find the RMS phase current and the RMS neutral current. Why does the fifth harmonic not reach the neutral?

### Problem 07-11.9 [B]

**Reading two wattmeters.** On a balanced three-wire load with positive sequence, wattmeter 1 (current coil in line a, voltage coil from a to b) reads −2 kW and wattmeter 2 (line c, voltage from c to b) reads 10 kW. Find $P$, $Q$ and the power factor. At what power factor does one reading pass through zero?

### Problem 07-11.10 [B]

**Correcting a plant.** A 480 V, 60 Hz plant draws 300 kW at power factor 0.75 lagging. Find the capacitive reactive power for 0.95 lagging, the capacitance per phase of a Δ-connected bank, and the line current before and after.

### Problem 07-11.11 [C]

**Why three phases run smoothly.** Example 07-11.1's load absorbs 12.8 kW at power factor 0.8. (a) If the same power were delivered by a single-phase load at the same power factor, between what values would the instantaneous power swing? (b) Show that the three-phase instantaneous power is constant. (c) Why does that matter for a generator or motor?

### Problem 07-11.12 [C]

**Distribution voltage and cable loss.** A 17.3 kW rack (unity power factor) is fed through 30 m of three-phase cable whose conductors each have 2 mΩ/m. (a) Find the cable loss at 415 V and at 208 V. (b) Recompute the four rows of the vendor table discussed in §9 (three-phase 208 V at 30 A continuous, three-phase 208 V at 60 A full, three-phase 415 V at 30 A full, single-phase 208 V at 30 A full) and say which of the vendor's figures are wrong.

### Problem 07-11.13 [B]

**Negative sequence.** A balanced Y load of $8 + j6$ Ω per phase is connected to a 400 V supply whose sequence is negative (acb). Take $\mathbf{V}_{an}$ at 0°. (a) Find $\mathbf{V}_{ab}$ and $\mathbf{I}_a$. (b) Find the two wattmeter readings (current coils in lines a and c, voltage coils to b), their sum, and $\sqrt3(W_2 - W_1)$. (c) Compare with the load's actual $Q$ and with the readings for positive sequence. What goes wrong if the sequence is assumed rather than checked?

### Problem 07-11.14 [B]

**An unbalanced Δ load from a Δ source.** A Δ-connected generator supplies 400 V line voltages with positive sequence ($\mathbf{V}_{ab} = 400\angle0°$ V). The Δ load has $Z_{ab}$ = 100 Ω, $Z_{bc}$ = j100 Ω and $Z_{ca}$ = −j100 Ω. (a) Find the three phase currents and the three line currents. (b) Find the total real and reactive power. (c) Find the equivalent Y source voltage $\mathbf{V}_{an}$ that a per-phase model of this generator would use.
