# Problems 04-11 · Introduction to Solid-State Physics

**Tiers:** **[A]** concept/recall · **[B]** standard exam level · **[C]** challenge/design.
**Conventions:** $h = 6.62607015\times10^{-34}$ J·s, $\hbar = h/2\pi$, $e = 1.602176634\times10^{-19}$ C, $k = 1.380649\times10^{-23}$ J/K (all exact); $m_0 = 9.1093837139\times10^{-31}$ kg; $R = 8.314462618$ J/(mol·K); $hc$ = 1239.84 eV·nm; $kT$ = 25.85 meV at 300 K. Free-electron formulas use $m_0$ unless an effective mass is given. Answers to 4 significant figures unless exact.

Suggested approach: do all [A] and [B] problems before looking at any solution. Attempt each [C] problem for at least 20 minutes before reading its solution.

## Problems

### Problem 04-11.1 [A]

**The germanium crystal.** Germanium has the diamond structure with lattice constant 0.5658 nm (take as given). Find (a) its atomic density in cm⁻³ and (b) its nearest-neighbor distance.

### Problem 04-11.2 [A]

**Reading Fermi–Dirac.** At 300 K find the occupation probability of a state (a) $3kT$ above and (b) $3kT$ below the Fermi level. (c) How far above $E_F$ (in eV) must a state lie for its occupation to fall to 1%?

### Problem 04-11.3 [B]

**Aluminum's Fermi sea.** Aluminum contributes 3 conduction electrons per atom, $n = 1.81\times10^{29}$ m⁻³ (take as given). Find (a) $E_F$, (b) $v_F$ and (c) $T_F$.

### Problem 04-11.4 [B]

**Sodium's density of states.** Sodium has $n = 2.65\times10^{28}$ m⁻³ (take as given). Find (a) $E_F$, (b) the density of states at $E_F$ per eV per cm³, using $g(E_F)/V = 3n/(2E_F)$, and (c) the number of electrons per cm³ in an energy window $kT$ wide at $E_F$ at 300 K.

### Problem 04-11.5 [B]

**From a bond to a band.** Two identical atoms have a level at $E_0 = 0$ with coupling $t$ = 1.5 eV. (a) How much does putting two electrons in the bonding state lower the energy? (b) For an open chain of 6 such atoms, whose levels are $E_0 - 2t\cos\big(m\pi/(N+1)\big)$, $m = 1, \dots, N$, find the lowest and highest levels. (c) What band width does a very long chain approach?

### Problem 04-11.6 [B]

**Measuring an effective mass.** Near a conduction-band minimum, a state 10.0 meV above the edge has wavenumber $2.0\times10^8$ m⁻¹ measured from the minimum. Assuming a parabolic band, find (a) $m^*/m_0$ and (b) the acceleration of an electron at the band bottom in a field of 1 kV/cm (ignoring collisions).

### Problem 04-11.7 [C]

**Why Bloch oscillations are rare.** A band $E = E_0 - 2t\cos ka$ has $t$ = 0.5 eV and $a$ = 0.3 nm. (a) Find the maximum group velocity. (b) In a field of 1 kV/cm, how long does an electron starting at $k = 0$ take to reach the zone edge? (c) How many collision times of 0.1 ps is that?

### Problem 04-11.8 [A]

**Counting bands.** (a) How many bands do silicon's valence electrons fill? (b) What fraction of its conduction band does sodium (one valence electron per atom, one atom per primitive cell) fill? (c) How many electron states (including spin) does one band hold in 1 cm³ of silicon?

### Problem 04-11.9 [B]

**Silicon's intrinsic carriers versus temperature.** Use the fit $n_i(T) = 5.29\times10^{19}(T/300)^{2.54}e^{-6726/T}$ cm⁻³ to find $n_i$ at (a) 300 K, (b) 350 K and (c) 400 K. (d) At what temperature does $n_i$ reach $10^{14}$ cm⁻³, 1% of a typical $10^{16}$ cm⁻³ doping?

### Problem 04-11.10 [B]

**Gaps and light.** (a) What band gap emits at 450 nm (a blue InGaN LED)? (b) Find the absorption cut-off wavelength of β-Ga₂O₃ (4.9 eV). (c) Find the photon energy at 1310 nm; which of Ge (0.67 eV), Si (1.12 eV) and GaAs (1.42 eV) can detect it? (d) Find the wavenumber of a photon at silicon's absorption edge (1.12 eV) and (e) its ratio to π/a ($a$ = 0.543102 nm), the scale of silicon's Brillouin zone.

### Problem 04-11.11 [C]

**Following a hole.** A valence band near its maximum is $E = E_v - \hbar^2k^2/(2m_h)$ with $m_h = 0.5\,m_0$ (take as given). The electron in the state at $k = 1.0\times10^9$ m⁻¹ is missing. Find (a) the velocity of that state, (b) the hole's velocity, and (c) the hole's energy measured below $E_v$ (that is, $E_v - E$). (d) In which direction does the hole contribute current?

### Problem 04-11.12 [C]

**Sommerfeld for aluminum.** Using Problem 04-11.3's $T_F$ and 3 conduction electrons per atom, find at 300 K (a) the electronic heat capacity per mole of aluminum and (b) its ratio to the lattice's $3R$.

### Problem 04-11.13 [C]

**A Bragg gap.** A 1-D lattice of period 0.4 nm has potential $V(x) = 2U\cos(2\pi x/a)$ with $U$ = 0.3 eV. Find (a) the free-electron energy at the zone edge, (b) and (c) the first-order energies of the two standing waves, and (d) the electron wavelength there.

### Problem 04-11.14 [A]

**How dilute is doping?** Using silicon's atomic density from Example 04-11.1, find the ratio of dopant atoms to silicon atoms for (a) $10^{16}$ cm⁻³ and (b) $10^{19}$ cm⁻³.

### Problem 04-11.15 [A]

**Reading a market forecast.** The chapter reports forecasts of $11 billion in 2031 at a 20% CAGR from 2025 for power SiC, and $3.5 billion in 2031 at a 35% CAGR for power GaN. Find the 2025 market sizes these imply (derived, not reported).
