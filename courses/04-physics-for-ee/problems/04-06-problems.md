# Problems 04-06 · Magnetic Fields and Forces

Chapter: [04-06](../chapters/04-06-magnetic-fields-and-forces.md) · Worked solutions: [04-06 solutions](../solutions/04-06-solutions.md)

**Tiers:** **[A]** concept/recall · **[B]** standard exam level · **[C]** challenge/design.
**Conventions:** $e = 1.602176634\times10^{-19}$ C; $m_e = 9.1093837139\times10^{-31}$ kg; $m_p = 1.67262192595\times10^{-27}$ kg; 1 u = 1.66054×10⁻²⁷ kg; $\mu_0 = 1.25663706127\times10^{-6}$ N/A². Copper has $8.491\times10^{28}$ conduction electrons per m³. Answers to 4 significant figures unless exact.

Suggested approach: do all [A] and [B] problems before looking at any solution. Attempt each [C] problem for at least 20 minutes before reading its solution.

## Problems

### Problem 04-06.1 [A]

**Direction and size.** A proton moves along $+x$ at $2\times10^5$ m/s through a 0.2 T field lying in the $xy$-plane at 30° from the $x$-axis. Find the magnitude and direction of the force.

### Problem 04-06.2 [A]

**Units.** (a) Express a 50 µT field in gauss. (b) Find the field 1 cm from a long wire carrying 10 A, in µT and in gauss.

### Problem 04-06.3 [A]

**A cyclotron.** (a) Find the cyclotron frequency of a proton in a 1.5 T field. (b) Find the radius of its orbit when it has 1 MeV of kinetic energy (non-relativistic).

### Problem 04-06.4 [B]

**A velocity selector.** Crossed fields of $10^5$ V/m and 0.05 T pass only one speed. (a) Find it. (b) Through what voltage must electrons be accelerated from rest to have that speed?

### Problem 04-06.5 [B]

**Isotope separation.** Singly charged ¹⁰B⁺ and ¹¹B⁺ ions are accelerated through 30 kV into a 0.8 T field. Find each radius and their separation after a half circle.

### Problem 04-06.6 [A]

**Force on a wire.** A 0.5 m straight wire carrying 10 A lies at 60° to a 0.2 T field. Find the force on it.

### Problem 04-06.7 [B]

**A coil in a field.** A 50-turn rectangular coil of 3 cm × 4 cm carries 0.2 A in a 0.3 T field, with its normal at 30° to the field. Find (a) its magnetic moment, (b) the torque, and (c) the energy difference between aligned and anti-aligned orientations.

### Problem 04-06.8 [B]

**A finite segment.** Find the field 2 cm from the midpoint of a 10 cm straight segment carrying 5 A, and compare it with an infinitely long wire.

### Problem 04-06.9 [B]

**Antiparallel wires.** Two long parallel wires 4 cm apart carry 3 A and 5 A in opposite directions. Find (a) the force per meter between them, and whether it attracts or repels, and (b) the field midway between them.

### Problem 04-06.10 [B]

**Solenoid and toroid design.** (a) A solenoid 30 cm long with 600 turns must produce 10 mT. What current does it need? (b) A toroid of 500 turns with inner radius 4 cm and outer radius 6 cm carries 2 A. Find the field at its inner and outer radii.

### Problem 04-06.11 [B]

**Inside a wire.** A wire of radius 1 mm carries 10 A spread uniformly. Find the field at 0.5 mm, 1 mm and 5 mm from its axis.

### Problem 04-06.12 [B]

**Choosing a Hall material.** (a) Find the Hall voltage across a copper strip 0.1 mm thick carrying 10 A in 0.5 T. (b) Find it for an n-type silicon plate 5 µm thick with $10^{15}$ electrons per cm³ carrying 0.5 mA in 50 mT. (c) Find the silicon plate's Hall coefficient.

### Problem 04-06.13 [C]

**Characterizing a wafer.** A Hall measurement on a p-type silicon sample 0.5 mm thick, with 1 mA in 0.1 T, gives a Hall voltage of +2.50 mV (positive in the sense expected for holes). Its resistivity is 27.7 Ω·cm. Find (a) the hole density in cm⁻³ and (b) the hole mobility.

### Problem 04-06.14 [C]

**Rejecting stray fields.** A current of 10 A flows out along one leg of a U-shaped conductor and back along the other. Two Hall plates sit 1 mm from the two legs (each plate near one leg; treat each leg as a long wire and neglect the farther leg). A uniform stray field of 0.5 mT from a nearby motor also passes through both plates. (a) Find the current's field at each plate (give signs). (b) Find the differential signal (plate 1 minus plate 2). (c) What error would the stray field cause in a single-plate sensor, as a percentage of its signal?

### Problem 04-06.15 [C]

**Gapping a core.** The ferrite core of Example 04-06.8 (mean path 5 cm, $\mu_r$ = 2000, 20 turns, $B_s$ = 0.39 T at 100 °C) is given a 0.5 mm air gap. Using $\oint\vec H\cdot d\vec l = NI$ with $B$ continuous across the gap, find (a) the flux density per ampere, (b) the saturation current at 100 °C, and (c) how many times larger it is than without the gap. What is the cost?

### Problem 04-06.16 [C]

**A loop as a dipole.** (a) Show that a loop's on-axis field $\mu_0IR^2/[2(R^2 + z^2)^{3/2}]$ becomes $\mu_0m/(2\pi z^3)$ for $z \gg R$, with $m = I\pi R^2$. (b) For $R$ = 1 cm, $I$ = 1 A and $z$ = 10 cm, find the exact field, the dipole approximation, and their ratio.

### Problem 04-06.17 [B]

**A wire with non-uniform current.** A long straight wire of radius $a$ = 1.0 mm carries a total current of 3.0 A, with current density growing linearly from the axis, $J(r) = J_0\,r/a$. (a) Find $J_0$. (b) Use Ampère's law to find $B$ at $r$ = 0.5 mm, inside the wire. (c) Find $B$ at $r$ = 2.0 mm, outside it. (d) How does $B$ grow with $r$ inside, and why does it differ from a uniform wire's?
