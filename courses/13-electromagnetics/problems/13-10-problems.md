# Problems 13-10 · Waveguides and Resonators

Chapter: [13-10](../chapters/13-10-waveguides-and-resonators.md) · Worked solutions: [13-10 solutions](../solutions/13-10-solutions.md)

**Tiers:** **[A]** concept/recall · **[B]** standard exam level · **[C]** challenge/design.
**Conventions:** $c$ = 2.998 × 10⁸ m/s; $\eta_0$ = 376.7 Ω; copper σ = 5.8 × 10⁷ S/m; guides are air-filled unless stated; WR-90 is 22.86 mm × 10.16 mm; fibre indices are illustrative.

Suggested approach: do all [A] and [B] problems before looking at any solution. Attempt each [C] problem for at least 20 minutes before reading its solution.

---

## Problems

### Problem 13-10.1 [A]

**An S-band guide.** WR-284 is 72.136 mm wide. Find its TE₁₀ cutoff frequency.

### Problem 13-10.2 [A]

**TE₁₀ at 12 GHz.** For WR-90 at 12 GHz, find the guide wavelength and the wave impedance.

### Problem 13-10.3 [A]

**Fill it with plastic.** WR-90 is filled with a dielectric of $\varepsilon_r$ = 2.25. Find the new TE₁₀ cutoff.

### Problem 13-10.4 [B]

**Dispersion over a metre.** Find the group delay through 1 m of WR-90 at 9 GHz and at 11 GHz, and the difference. What does this mean for a short pulse?

### Problem 13-10.5 [B]

**A microwave-oven door.** A door screen has round-ish holes about 3 mm across in a 1 mm thick metal sheet. Treating each hole as a 3 mm square waveguide below cutoff at 2.45 GHz, find α and the attenuation through the sheet's thickness. Why does the real screen do better than this estimate?

### Problem 13-10.6 [B]

**A cube.** Find the TE₁₀₁ resonant frequency of an air-filled cubic cavity 5 cm on a side. Which other modes resonate at the same frequency?

### Problem 13-10.7 [B]

**The cube's Q.** Find the conductor Q of the copper cube of Problem 13-10.6 in its TE₁₀₁ mode, and the surface resistance at resonance.

### Problem 13-10.8 [C]

**Where is the loss lowest?** For copper WR-90 in the TE₁₀ mode, find the frequency at which the conductor attenuation is smallest and its value. Is that frequency inside the single-mode band?

### Problem 13-10.9 [B]

**A multimode fibre.** A fibre has a 25 µm core radius and NA = 0.2. Find V at 850 nm and estimate the number of guided modes with the large-V rule $V^2/2$.

### Problem 13-10.10 [B]

**Design for single-mode.** For NA = 0.12, find the largest core radius that keeps a step-index fibre single-mode at wavelengths of 1260 nm and above.

### Problem 13-10.11 [C]

**Power handling.** For TE₁₀ with peak field $E_0$ at the centre of the guide, the power is $P = E_0^2ab/(4Z_{TE})$. Derive this, and find the maximum power WR-90 can carry at 10 GHz if air breaks down at 3 MV/m.

### Problem 13-10.12 [A]

**Near cutoff.** Find the TE₁₀ wave impedance of WR-90 at 7 GHz, and explain why operating this close to cutoff is avoided.

### Problem 13-10.13 [C]

**A coupled cavity.** The cavity of Example 13-10.5 (Q = 7838 at 9.273 GHz) is coupled to a feed line so that the external Q equals the unloaded Q (critical coupling). Find the loaded Q and the 3 dB bandwidth.

### Problem 13-10.14 [C]

**How much light is in the core?** For the fibre of Example 13-10.6 at 1310 nm, integrate the LP₀₁ intensity to find the fraction of the power inside the core.
