# History Timeline

Key inventions, theories and industry inflection points in electrical engineering and semiconductors. Each entry is stated in the chapter linked beside it, where the idea is taught and its source is cited; the dates here follow those chapters.

| Year | Event | Chapter |
|---|---|---|
| 1748 | Euler publishes the relation $e^{jx} = \cos x + j\sin x$ (in *Introductio in analysin infinitorum*) | [02-01](../courses/02-complex-numbers-des-and-linear-algebra/chapters/02-01-complex-numbers-euler-and-phasors.md) |
| 1831 | Michael Faraday finds that a changing magnetic flux induces a voltage | [04-07](../courses/04-physics-for-ee/chapters/04-07-faraday-lenz-and-inductance.md) |
| 1853 | Hermann von Helmholtz states the equivalent-source theorem; Léon Charles Thévenin publishes it in 1883, and it carries his name | [06-08](../courses/06-circuit-analysis-1-dc/chapters/06-08-thevenin-and-norton.md) |
| 1854 | George Boole turns logical reasoning into algebra | [08-02](../courses/08-digital-logic-design/chapters/08-02-boolean-algebra-and-gates.md) |
| 1861–1865 | James Clerk Maxwell adds the displacement current (1861–62) and shows that his equations predict electromagnetic waves (1865) | [04-08](../courses/04-physics-for-ee/chapters/04-08-maxwell-preview.md) |
| 1893 | Charles Proteus Steinmetz presents complex-number (phasor) methods for AC circuit analysis | [02-01](../courses/02-complex-numbers-des-and-linear-algebra/chapters/02-01-complex-numbers-euler-and-phasors.md) |
| 1895–1897 | Tokyo adopts a 50 Hz generator (1895) and Osaka a 60 Hz one (1897), splitting Japan's grid frequency to this day | [07-11](../courses/07-circuit-analysis-2-transient-and-ac/chapters/07-11-three-phase-circuits.md) |
| 1900 | Paul Drude models a metal's electrons as a gas of particles that accelerate in a field and collide | [04-05](../courses/04-physics-for-ee/chapters/04-05-current-resistance-and-conduction.md) |
| 1908 | W. S. Gosset ("Student") derives the t-distribution for small-sample quality control | [03-05](../courses/03-probability-statistics-and-random-variables/chapters/03-05-estimation-and-hypothesis-testing.md) |
| 1922–1926 | J. R. Carson recasts Heaviside's operational calculus as an integral equation of the Laplace type | [07-04](../courses/07-circuit-analysis-2-transient-and-ac/chapters/07-04-laplace-domain-circuit-analysis.md) |
| 1928 | Nyquist derives the thermal-noise power of a resistor from a thermodynamic argument, and the condition for sending pulses without intersymbol interference | [12-10](../courses/12-electronics-2/chapters/12-10-noise-in-circuits.md), [17-04](../courses/17-communication-systems/chapters/17-04-baseband-digital-transmission.md) |
| 1930 | Stephen Butterworth publishes the maximally flat filter | [07-08](../courses/07-circuit-analysis-2-transient-and-ac/chapters/07-08-filters-and-resonance.md) |
| 1938 | Claude Shannon's master's thesis shows that Boolean algebra describes networks of relay switches | [08-02](../courses/08-digital-logic-design/chapters/08-02-boolean-algebra-and-gates.md) |
| 1940 | Bode relates a loop's gain slope to its phase, pricing the bandwidth that feedback costs | [11-08](../courses/11-signals-and-systems/chapters/11-08-laplace-transform-and-system-functions.md) |
| 1948 | Claude Shannon defines channel capacity, the limit no communication scheme can beat | [17-07](../courses/17-communication-systems/chapters/17-07-information-theory-and-shannon.md) |
| 1948 | W. R. Evans introduces the root locus for sketching closed-loop poles by hand | [15-06](../courses/15-control-systems/chapters/15-06-root-locus.md) |
| 1955 | R. P. Sallen and E. L. Key publish the one-op-amp active filter that bears their names | [07-08](../courses/07-circuit-analysis-2-transient-and-ac/chapters/07-08-filters-and-resonance.md) |
| 1965 | Gordon Moore observes that the number of components per chip at minimum cost is doubling yearly; he revises the rate in 1975 | [09-11](../courses/09-semiconductor-devices/chapters/09-11-cmos-and-scaling.md) |
| 1965 | Cooley and Tukey publish the fast Fourier transform | [16-04](../courses/16-digital-signal-processing/chapters/16-04-the-fft.md) |
| 1965 | Kurokawa defines power waves, the basis of S-parameters with complex reference impedances | [21-01](../courses/21-rf-and-microwave-engineering/chapters/21-01-s-parameters.md) |
| 1968 | Melvin Conway states that system designs mirror the communication structure of the organizations that build them | [26-06](../courses/26-engineering-design-and-professional-practice/chapters/26-06-team-structures-and-sizing.md) |
| 1973 | Liu and Layland prove rate-monotonic scheduling optimal for independent periodic tasks on one core | [18-07](../courses/18-embedded-systems-and-microcontrollers/chapters/18-07-rtos-concepts.md) |
| 1974 | Dennard and colleagues show how to scale a MOSFET at constant field, keeping power density constant | [09-11](../courses/09-semiconductor-devices/chapters/09-11-cmos-and-scaling.md) |
| 1975 | Sokal and Sokal introduce the class-E switching power amplifier | [21-07](../courses/21-rf-and-microwave-engineering/chapters/21-07-power-amplifiers.md) |
| 1984 | Verilog is created at Gateway Design Automation; it becomes IEEE 1364 in 1995 | [08-05](../courses/08-digital-logic-design/chapters/08-05-verilog-and-testbenches.md) |
| 1986 | Bryant proves that reduced ordered binary decision diagrams are canonical | [19-13](../courses/19-digital-vlsi-design/chapters/19-13-verification-methods.md) |
| 1987 | TSMC pioneers the pure-play foundry, the split between fabless design and manufacturing | [25-05](../courses/25-fabrication-packaging-test-and-reliability/chapters/25-05-foundries-pdks-specialty-processes.md) |
| 1993 | Turbo codes approach the Shannon limit, followed by rediscovered LDPC codes (mid-1990s) and polar codes (2009) | [17-07](../courses/17-communication-systems/chapters/17-07-information-theory-and-shannon.md) |
| 1995 | Wulf and McKee name the memory wall | [23-01](../courses/23-memory-and-storage-technology/chapters/23-01-memory-hierarchy-and-memory-wall.md) |
| 1996 | Kocher shows that the time cryptographic operations take can reveal their keys | [18-11](../courses/18-embedded-systems-and-microcontrollers/chapters/18-11-hardware-security.md) |
| 1999 | Hu's Berkeley group publishes the FinFET, after a 1996 DARPA call for switches beyond 25 nm | [09-12](../courses/09-semiconductor-devices/chapters/09-12-finfet-gaa-cfet-backside-power.md) |
| 2017 | Google publishes the first analysis of its Tensor Processing Unit in a datacenter (ISCA 2017) | [14-14](../courses/14-computer-organization-and-architecture/chapters/14-14-ai-accelerators.md) |

Later and perishable industry events (product launches, node introductions, market shifts) are dated and sourced in the [research log](research-log.md) and in the [case studies](../practitioner/case-studies/README.md), not here.
