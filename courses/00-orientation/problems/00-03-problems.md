## Problems

### Problem 00-03.1 [A]

Which two learning techniques did Dunlosky et al. (2013) rate as "high utility"? Name three they rated "low utility". Which of the low-utility techniques have you relied on most in past study?

### Problem 00-03.2 [A]

Express the volt, ohm, farad and henry in SI base units (kg, m, s, A). Start from W = J/s, J = kg·m²·s⁻², V = W/A, Ω = V/A, C = A·s, F = C/V and H = V·s/A.

### Problem 00-03.3 [A]

Check the units of each formula and say whether it can be correct:

(a) $\tau = L/R$  (b) $E = \tfrac{1}{2}LI^2$  (c) $f = 1/(2\pi RC)$  (d) a "wave speed" $v = \sqrt{L/C}$  (e) a "power" $P = I/R$

### Problem 00-03.4 [B]

A 12 V battery with 0.5 Ω internal resistance supplies a 5.5 Ω load. Find the current, the load voltage, the load power and the efficiency (load power ÷ battery power). Then perform the limit check $R_{int} \to 0$ and the magnitude check.

### Problem 00-03.5 [B]

A chip has 50 billion transistors clocked at 2 GHz, with average activity 1% and 0.3 fJ per switch. (a) Estimate the dynamic power. (b) What is the average power per transistor? (c) Does the answer seem plausible for a large processor? Name the assumption you are least sure of.

### Problem 00-03.6 [B]

With a first review interval of 1 day, how many successful reviews does it take for the cumulative time to exceed 365 days if each interval is multiplied by (a) 2.0 or (b) 3.0? What does a higher ease factor trade away?

### Problem 00-03.7 [B]

Course 01 (Calculus) is budgeted at about 116 hours. How many weeks does it take at 11 hours per week? At 8 hours per week? What study-loop step would you protect first if your weekly hours fall?

### Problem 00-03.8 [B]

In Roediger & Karpicke (2006), one-week recall was 61% for mostly-testing and 40% for rereading only. Compute the absolute difference (percentage points) and the relative improvement (%). Which number would a marketer of a study product quote, and why?

### Problem 00-03.9 [C] (find the errors)

A learner's notes contain three formulas. Use units to find which are wrong and suggest the likely correct form:

(a) energy in a capacitor $E = CV/2$  (b) RL time constant $\tau = R/L$  (c) Ohm's law $I = VR$

### Problem 00-03.10 [C] (a forgetting model)

Model recall as $R(t) = e^{-t/S}$ with stability $S = 5$ days.

(a) What fraction is recalled after 7 days with no review?
(b) If you review when $R$ falls to 90%, when is the first review?
(c) After a successful review, $S$ becomes 2.5 times larger. When is the next review (again at 90%)?
(d) Explain why this model implies expanding intervals.
