**Tiers:** **[A]** concept/recall · **[B]** standard exam level · **[C]** challenge/design.
**Conventions:** $h = 6.62607015\times10^{-34}$ J·s, $\hbar = h/2\pi$, $c = 299\,792\,458$ m/s, $e = 1.602176634\times10^{-19}$ C (all exact); $m_e = 9.1093837139\times10^{-31}$ kg; $\varepsilon_0 = 8.8541878188\times10^{-12}$ F/m; $hc$ = 1239.84 eV·nm. ## Problems

### Problem 04-10.1 [A]

**Laser photons.** Find the photon energy of a 405 nm (Blu-ray), 650 nm (red) and 940 nm (infrared proximity sensor) laser.

### Problem 04-10.2 [A]

**A photodiode's current.** 10 µW of 850 nm light falls on a photodiode with 70% quantum efficiency. Find the photon rate, the photocurrent and the responsivity.

### Problem 04-10.3 [B]

**Measuring Planck's constant.** A metal gives stopping voltages of 0.605 V under 450 nm light and 1.247 V under 365 nm light. Find (a) $h/e$ from the slope, (b) $h$, and (c) the work function.

### Problem 04-10.4 [B]

**Electron and neutron wavelengths.** (a) Find the de Broglie wavelength of electrons in a 30 kV scanning electron microscope (ignore the small relativistic correction). (b) Find that of a thermal neutron with 0.025 eV (neutron mass 1.675×10⁻²⁷ kg). Why can both probe crystal structure?

### Problem 04-10.5 [B]

**A thin channel.** An electron is confined to a 5 nm-thick transistor channel. Estimate its minimum momentum spread across the channel (Δx = 5 nm) and the corresponding confinement energy $(\Delta p)^2/2m$, using the free-electron mass.

### Problem 04-10.6 [B]

**A GaAs quantum well.** Model a 5 nm GaAs quantum well as an infinite well with effective mass 0.067 $m_0$ (take as given). Find $E_1$, $E_2$, the spacing, and the wavelength of the 2→1 transition.

### Problem 04-10.7 [B]

**Where is the particle?** For a particle in an infinite well of width $L$, find the probability of finding it in the middle third ($L/3 < x < 2L/3$) in (a) the ground state and (b) the $n = 2$ state. (c) Explain (b) using the shape of $|\psi_2|^2$.

### Problem 04-10.8 [A]

**The red hydrogen line.** Using $E_n = -13.61\ \text{eV}/n^2$ (Example 04-10.6), find the energy and wavelength of the photon emitted in the 3→2 transition.

### Problem 04-10.9 [C]

**The scanning tunneling microscope.** An electron at a metal's Fermi level tunnels across a vacuum gap whose barrier is the work function, 4.5 eV above it. (a) Find κ. (b) By what factor does the tunneling probability change per 0.1 nm of gap? Why does this let an STM image single atoms?

### Problem 04-10.10 [C]

**A leakage specification.** For the barrier of Example 04-10.7 (1 eV electron, 3.1 eV barrier, free-electron mass), what thickness gives a transmission of exactly 10⁻¹⁰?

### Problem 04-10.11 [C]

**Shot noise in a pixel.** During one exposure an image-sensor pixel receives 1.806×10⁻¹⁷ J of 550 nm light, with 60% quantum efficiency. Find (a) the number of photons, (b) the mean number of photoelectrons, and (c) the signal-to-noise ratio set by Poisson (shot) noise, $\sqrt N$ (03-06).

### Problem 04-10.12 [C]

**Quantum-dot color.** Model a quantum dot as a cube of side 4 nm with infinite walls, an electron effective mass of 0.13 $m_0$, and a bulk band gap of 1.74 eV (take as given; ignore the hole's confinement). (a) Find the ground-state confinement energy $3h^2/(8m^*L^2)$. (b) Find the emitted photon's energy and wavelength. (c) Which way does the color move if the dot grows?

### Problem 04-10.13 [B]

**Measuring a qubit.** A qubit is in the state $\tfrac12|0\rangle + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}i|1\rangle$. (a) Find the probabilities of reading 0 and 1. (b) In 1000 identical runs, find the expected number of 1s and its standard deviation (03-02's binomial distribution).

### Problem 04-10.14 [A]

**Which light breaks bonds?** A carbon–carbon bond takes about 3.6 eV to break (take as given). (a) Find the longest wavelength whose single photons can break it. (b) How many times the energy of a 5 GHz photon is that?
